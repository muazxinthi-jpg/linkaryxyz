Four states. A clear view of the evidence.

Every claim should show what supports it.

Manual

Entered by a person

Person-entered information that has not been independently confirmed.

Tracked

Recorded by Linkary

A first-party Linkary event, such as a tracking-link event.

Correlated

A supported connection

Signals support a possible connection, but do not prove attribution.

Verified

Supported by proof

Strong first-party or external proof supports the claim.

Manual entries, submissions, and estimates do not become verified automatically.

05 / Your questions

Frequently asked questions.

How does off-platform activity tracking work?

Use Linkary tracking links to connect recorded events to the relevant activity and contributor. You can also record work performed elsewhere. The evidence label shows what supports each record; not every off-platform action can be tracked or attributed.

How do the four evidence states differ?

Manual information is entered by a person. Tracked events are recorded by Linkary. Correlated signals support a possible connection without proving attribution. Verified claims have strong first-party or external proof.

Do campaigns have to run inside Linkary?

No. Run growth anywhere. Track it in Linkary. You can record work from external campaigns or use the Linkary workspace to coordinate a campaign here.

How does Controlled Beta access work?

Linkary is in Controlled Beta. Access follows the existing invitation and review process. Eligible beta users may receive access without payment while we validate real workflows.
