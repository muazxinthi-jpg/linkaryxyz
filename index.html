FAQ

Clear answers.

What is Linkary?+

Linkary is a growth attribution, identity, relationship intelligence and evidence platform for Web3 Projects, creators and communities.

Does my campaign need to run inside Linkary?+

No. Run growth anywhere and use Linkary as the evidence and attribution layer around the activity.

What does Verified mean?+

Verified means strong first-party or externally verified evidence supports the attribution. It is not limited to blockchain evidence.

How do profile auctions work?+

Eligible public profiles can offer selected sponsored banner inventory through timed auctions. Payment and delivery logic remain inside the Linkary application.

What are Usage Credits?+

Usage Credits are an operating allowance for eligible intelligence and provider-assisted features. They are not money or transferable tokens.
