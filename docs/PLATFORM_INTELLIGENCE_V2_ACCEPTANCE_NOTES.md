# Platform Intelligence V2 acceptance notes

Acceptance focuses on decision usefulness without weakening data integrity.

The release is acceptable when Superadmin can quickly answer:

- How many people are active daily, weekly and monthly?
- What share of registered users have activated a profile?
- What share are currently paid accounts?
- How much of current acquisition is referral-driven?
- Are users, referrals and verified revenue accelerating or slowing versus the previous 30 days?
- Who is approved for an internal referral reward, how much is outstanding and how long have they waited?
- How are monthly actuals tracking against explicitly set internal targets?
- What has happened to users, referrals and verified revenue across the last six calendar months?

The release must not answer unsupported questions such as CAC, LTV, burn, runway or historical MRR until Linkary has the required source ledgers.
